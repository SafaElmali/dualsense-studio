A

OBS Browser Source TRANSPARENT

  1. Copy your link. In OBS, add a Browser source and paste it into URL.
  2. Set width to 800, height to 600, and frame rate to 60.
  3. Right-click the source → Interact. Click the controller, then press a button on your DualSense to activate input.

Check that the buttons still respond after switching to your game. For touchpad finger tracking, or if input stops when switching apps, use the capture-window method.

B

Browser window capture DIRECT CONNECTION

  1. Choose Green or Blue above, then open the capture window in desktop Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click Connect DualSense and select your controller. Hide the setup panel once live input works.
  3. In OBS, capture that browser window, crop its borders, and add a Chroma Key filter matching your background.

Keep the capture window open and unminimized. Direct input continues when your game has focus. USB is the simplest connection.

    Is the DualSense OBS overlay free?

    Yes. Streamer mode is free to use, with no account or membership required. Tips through Buy Me a Coffee are optional.

    How do I add the controller overlay to OBS?

    Choose your look, select Copy OBS link, and paste it into an OBS Browser source. Start with a width of 800, a height of 600, and 60 frames per second. Open Interact and press a controller button to activate input. The setup guide above also explains browser window capture.

    Can I use a transparent background?

    Choose Clear and use an OBS Browser source for a transparent overlay. For browser window capture, choose Green or Blue, then add a matching Chroma Key filter in OBS.

    What can I customize?

    Set the camera angle and controller size, shell and light colors, button highlight color and opacity, and background. Show or hide stick arrows and L2/R2 trigger meters, then copy the OBS link to keep your settings.

    Can I preview the overlay without a controller?

    Yes. Select Try demo above the preview to see simulated button presses, stick movements, and trigger pressure. A connected controller is needed to show your own live inputs.

    Which controller and browser should I use?

    This overlay is designed for a PS5 DualSense connected over USB or Bluetooth. For touchpad finger tracking, or if OBS Browser source input stops when your game has focus, open the capture page in desktop Chrome or Edge and select Connect DualSense. Keep that window open and unminimized. You can also test your controller’s inputs in the studio.
